- Derive the three profile statistics from published listings, published community posts, and sold listings. Never show pending, rejected, or hidden content in public counts or lists.

- Show listing image, price, title, neighborhood, and sold status. Show post type, title, content, neighborhood, and an optional image.
- Use the Soft Cute cream and coral tokens, native Mini Program WXML/WXSS/JavaScript, rpx spacing, safe-area padding, and no emoji icons.
