# 社区动态

- Show the active neighborhood scope and let the user open the explicit location picker.
- When no neighborhood is selected, omit location parameters and show the complete unfiltered feed, announcements, and active-neighbor list.
- Show reviewed community posts only; published and sold listings remain in the home marketplace.
- Load post filters from active community-post categories so category configuration controls both labels and filters. Posts have authenticated likes and comments and can be shared.
- Keep new post publishing and moderation connected to the community feed, without adding community posts to the home item feed.
- Use the announcements, neighbor, like, comment, and favorite service results; do not invent activity counts.
- Community browsing filters are independent of home listing filters and do not join a community.
- Distinguish loading, retryable failure, and empty-post states with a useful next action.
- Use staggered finite card entrance motion and shimmer skeletons; honor reduced-motion preferences where supported.
